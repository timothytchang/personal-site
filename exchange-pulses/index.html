Minimum-time search varies both amplitudes and durations, refining through 8, 12 and 20 segments after pulse-order search. It verifies the whole target rotation up to global phase and keeps the shortest verified result. The lower bound is a speed limit, not necessarily attainable; “best found” is not proven optimal. Gate error is numerical average gate infidelity, not a device prediction.
